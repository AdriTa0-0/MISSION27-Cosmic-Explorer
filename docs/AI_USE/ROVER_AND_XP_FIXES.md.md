# Mission27 rover and XP update

- Replaced face-like toy rover with six-wheel lunar field rover (suspension arms, equipment, sensors, antenna and solar panel).
- Rover speed dial in the mission screen while driving: 20–100%, defaults to 65%; automatic speed assist keeps unsafe speeds under control.
- Forward max increases from 4 to 8 world units/sec before dial and safety restrictions.
- Drive with up/down and left/right or touch controls; Shift / Stop brakes.
- 800 XP cap enforced on all score awards and resumed saves. Mission report displays score / 800.

Note: Existing XP from tasks and emergencies may add to more than 800, but 800 is now the actual maximum credited and displayed. This keeps the existing gameplay tasks unchanged.
